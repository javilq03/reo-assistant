Reo Voice Preview

Listen to the Piper neural voices that Reo can download and run offline. Samples are the official ones (first paragraph of the Wikipedia “Rainbow” article). After you pick a favourite in Reo, it will be downloaded once into your voicebanks folder.

Official sample site: rhasspy.github.io/piper-samples
In Reo these appear under Reo’s Voice. Selecting a voice that isn’t installed yet starts the download automatically.
